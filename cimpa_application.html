Workshop Overview

Welcome to the application page for the upcoming CIMPA Research School on Algebraic and Enumerative Combinatorics. Applications are now open for the CIMPA Research School on Algebraic and Enumerative Combinatorics (Makerere University, Uganda). The school is intended for graduate students and early-career researchers interested in modern developments in algebraic and enumerative combinatorics. Participants will benefit from lecture courses delivered by internationally recognized experts, exercise sessions, and opportunities for scientific collaboration and networking. Please review the key dates and eligibility criteria before submitting your application.

✅ Meals and accommodation will be provided for all accepted participants.

✅ A limited number of travel grants will be available for participants from developing countries, subject to available funding.
